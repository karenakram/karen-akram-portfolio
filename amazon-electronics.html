Amazon Electronics — Product & Pricing Analysis

An exploratory analysis of Amazon's electronics catalog, delivered as a multi-page Power BI report for sales, marketing, delivery, and promotions.

Project Overview

This team project analyzed 42,675 Amazon electronics product listings across 15 categories. The work combined data preparation in Python with an interactive Power BI report that lets viewers explore revenue, ratings, delivery performance, and promotional activity.

PythonPandasNumPyPower BIExcel

Business Problem

Amazon's electronics catalog is large and varied. The analytical question: how do pricing, ratings, reviews, promotions, and delivery promises differ across product categories — and what does that reveal about sales performance?
